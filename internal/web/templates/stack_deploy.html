Deploy stack from compose

Supports a pragmatic subset of the compose format: image, command, environment, labels, ports, volumes (short syntax), networks, deploy.replicas/mode/placement/resources/update_config/restart_policy. Secrets and configs must already exist (create them under Secrets/Configs first) and are referenced with external: true. No build:, no bind-mount host paths resolved against your local machine.
