Single sign-on

Generic OIDC (OpenID Connect) - works with Okta, Authentik, Keycloak, Google Workspace, Azure AD, Auth0 and any other provider that exposes an issuer discovery document. Local username/password login stays available alongside SSO unless you turn on "Require SSO" below.

swarmdash fetches <issuer>/.well-known/openid-configuration to find the provider's authorization/token/userinfo endpoints.

Redirect URI to register with your identity provider: {{.RedirectURI}}

Access control
{{if .Config.EnforceSSO}}
Local password login is currently disabled - only "Sign in with {{.Config.Label}}" works on the login page, and POST /login rejects password attempts server-side too. If the identity provider becomes unreachable or misconfigured, every account is locked out until this is switched back off directly in the sso_config table/collection of the admin database (SQLite or MongoDB, whichever this instance is configured to use).
{{else}}

Off by default. Confirm SSO sign-in actually works end-to-end before turning this on - once enabled, the only way back in without direct database access is the identity provider itself.

{{end}}

Disable SSO
